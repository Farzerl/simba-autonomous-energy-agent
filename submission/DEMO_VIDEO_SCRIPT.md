# Two-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:15 — problem and promise

“SIMBA is an offline autonomous energy operations agent for African facilities. It
does more than predict a peak: it plans a safe response, asks for bounded approval,
tests actions in software, verifies the result and replans when reality changes.”

## 0:15–0:35 — show the operating boundary

Open the Agent tab. Point to **software-in-the-loop**, **live control disabled** and
the Energy Safety Firewall. Briefly show the six-step ‘What the agent does’ panel.

## 0:35–1:05 — run the complication

Choose **Device unavailable** and press **Run resilient demo**. Explain that the goal
is to stay below the configured campus peak limit without touching critical loads.
Show the initial plan, approval limits and the blocked unavailable device.

## 1:05–1:30 — show replanning and proof

Show the event timeline moving through observation, execution, response observation,
replanning and `TARGET_MET`. Point to verified demand, positive headroom, one replan,
zero critical-load actions and live control remaining disabled.

## 1:30–1:45 — show local language and offline value

Switch the interface to ChiShona, then isiNdebele. Explain that the language packs
are local and no facility data is sent to the cloud.

## 1:45–2:00 — close

“SIMBA combines forecasting with safe, auditable action on a normal CPU laptop. The
agent handles ambiguity; deterministic power-engineering logic remains authoritative.”

Keep the recording at or below two minutes. Avoid claims not backed by the visible
demo or final profiler output.
